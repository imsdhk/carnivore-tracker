Privacy Policy

Carnivore Tracker · Effective date: 2026-10-01

Summary

Carnivore Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

The health information you choose to log (for example weight, waist size, blood sugar, ketones, blood pressure, heart rate, mood, sleep, fasting, foods, medications, supplements, symptoms, notes and optional photos) and your app settings are saved only in local storage on your device. The developer has no server and no access to this data.

No collection, no sharing

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • The app does not send your data over the internet. It works fully offline.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Exports and backups you control

If you use Export JSON / CSV, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet or file saver. Where that file goes (for example a cloud drive, email or messaging app) is your choice and is governed by that service's privacy policy. Your device's own system backup (such as Android backup) may include app data according to your device settings; this is controlled by you and your device provider, not by the developer.

Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or similar permissions. If you choose to attach a photo, your device's file/photo picker is used and the selected image is compressed and stored locally with your entry.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in History, delete everything with Settings → Delete ALL data, or uninstall the app (which removes its local data from the device).

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Carnivore Tracker is a personal logging tool. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health, diet, fasting and medications.
